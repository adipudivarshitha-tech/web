Wednesday is a hit American supernatural mystery television series based on the character Wednesday Addams from The Addams Family. Directed in part by Tim Burton, the show stars Jenna Ortega as the titular character, who attempts to master her emerging psychic abilities, thwart a monstrous killing spree, and solve a 25-year-old supernatural mystery involving her parents at her new boarding school, Nevermore Academy.
